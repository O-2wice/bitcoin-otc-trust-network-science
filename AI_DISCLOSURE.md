# AI-use disclosure

Codex was used to research source and method references; draft the project plan, README, analysis scripts, explanatory notebooks, report text, figures, and presentation; and troubleshoot LaTeX, Jupyter, GitHub, and Cytoscape workflows. The report and presentation were based on computed outputs from the downloaded SNAP Bitcoin OTC dataset, not on generated numerical results.

Claude Code (Anthropic) was used to audit the repository against the project brief; fix reproducibility, documentation, and figure problems; write `scripts/theory_comparison.py`, the equal-volume and community-stability checks, and notebook 05; and revise the LaTeX report with those results. It was later used to add the degree-correlation results to the pipeline, checker and report; simplify the code and wording of the notebooks; restyle the Cytoscape figure and rebuild the CyAnimator video (`cytoscape/capture_in_cytoscape.py`, `cytoscape/compose_outputs.py`); rebuild the presentation; and write the project website in `docs/`.

Checks performed on the Codex-assisted work: the source SHA-256 and row-level audit were verified. Six numerical scripts were rerun in the pinned project environment using `scripts/reproduce.py`; the manifest records versions, script hashes, runtimes and output hashes. The checker passed, and previously reported numerical CSV values were unchanged within 1e-10. All four notebooks then in the repository executed, including worked graph examples and source-row recalculations. The review added annual-only and fixed-cohort comparisons, corrected the interpretation of isolates and community counts, and distinguished increasing accumulated inequality from yearly concentration. Before the later revisions, the LaTeX report compiled to 15 pages. Graph layouts, charts and the slide content were visually reviewed.

Checks performed on the Claude-assisted work: the full pipeline, now seven scripts, was rerun in the pinned environment on Windows and recorded in the manifest. Results from the earlier scripts were unchanged except for the row order of ties in one table. Separately written code reproduced the stored uniform and matched benchmark ratios to within 1e-9. The formula predictions were recomputed in notebook 05, the internal-link exponent was taken from Barabási's *Network Science* (Eq. 6.27) and checked by simulation, and the tail estimator was checked on simulated graphs. The audit found that most of the apparent fall in yearly concentration after 2013 is a volume effect, and the documents now say so. Numbers added to the LaTeX report were filled in from the stored outputs by a script, and its citations, labels, figures, and tables were checked automatically. The revised report was compiled with pdfLaTeX (MiKTeX) to 15 pages with no undefined references or lines running into the margin, and each page was checked visually. All notebooks executed without errors.

Checks performed on the later Claude-assisted work: after each change the eight-script pipeline was rerun and every stored CSV matched the previous run, and the manifest check passed. All six notebooks were executed from scratch, and their recalculated values were compared with the stored results. The report tables were compared programmatically with the result files, and the report, slides and website were checked visually. The website and slide charts are drawn from the stored CSVs by `docs/make_figures.py`, without new analysis.

The Cytoscape figure and video are drawn by Cytoscape 3.10.4 and animated by CyAnimator 2.2.1; node positions come from a deterministic Kamada–Kawai layout. CyAnimator occasionally mis-renders single frames, so the animation was recorded three times (121 frames each). For each frame the recording closest to the median of the three was kept, and six frames that still differed from both neighbours were omitted, leaving 115 frames. The year-end keyframes were inspected against the stated rating counts (5, 16, 54, 73 and 76). Frame and movie hashes are recorded in `outputs/cytoscape/cyanimator_provenance.json`. The local ELTE PowerPoint embeds this movie and contains speaker notes. The node-removal experiment uses 30 random replicates; model comparisons include eight degree-preserving rewires. The numerical manifest does not establish independent scientific validation or pixel-identical rebuilding of the presentation. The team must review and understand the code, citations, analytical choices, figures and claims before submission. AI assistance does not replace that review.
